Symphony is a free music app for Windows, Android and macOS. No ads, no subscription, no account, and offline downloads at no cost.

Why people switch

  • No ads, ever. Nothing interrupts the music between songs.
  • Free offline downloads. Save songs or whole playlists and listen on a plane, the subway or anywhere with no signal.
  • No subscription. Every feature is free. There is no premium tier to unlock.
  • Keep your playlists. Paste a playlist link from the app you use today and Symphony rebuilds it.

Free Spotify alternative

On Spotify's free plan you hear ads, can't download music for offline listening, and on mobile you often can't pick the exact song you want. Symphony lets you play any song you search for, download it, and skip as much as you like, all for free. Your existing Spotify playlists come with you: paste the link and keep listening.

Free Apple Music alternative

Apple Music has no free plan. Symphony gives you on-demand listening, offline downloads and synced lyrics without a subscription, and it runs on Windows, Android and macOS. Paste an Apple Music playlist link to bring your library over.

Everything else you get

  • Jam with friends: everyone hears the same song at the same moment, on the same Wi-Fi or online.
  • Synced lyrics that follow along as the song plays.
  • Made for you mixes and yearly and monthly listening recaps.
  • Desktop extras: media keys, system tray, keyboard shortcuts and Discord status.
  • A Discord bot that plays music in your voice channel.

Questions

Is Symphony really free?

Yes. Symphony has no subscription, no paid tier and no ads. It is a free, open-source personal project.

Can I download music to listen offline for free?

Yes. Download single songs or whole playlists and play them with no connection. Songs you play are cached too.

Can I bring my Spotify or Apple Music playlists?

Yes. Paste a public playlist link and Symphony rebuilds the playlist so you can keep listening.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your library and listening history stay on your device.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not on the App Store yet. iPhone users can sideload an early version.
